### Fixed

- **An in-place repair follows device-mapper and md layers from a journal to the disk it repairs (#952).** `repair_journal::check_journal_location` related the journal's device and the target only directly, so a journal on `/dev/dm-0` or `/dev/md0` built on the disk being repaired was accepted. It now follows each device down through `/sys/class/block/<name>/slaves` on Linux, recursively and a partition of a layered disk through its disk, and refuses when a device at the bottom of one stack is the same as, a partition of, or the disk holding a device at the bottom of the other, naming both devices. The resolution is the pure `repair_journal::layered_refusal` over a lookup of the devices under a device, and `repair_journal::sysfs_lower_devices` reads that lookup from sysfs. APFS containers on macOS are not followed yet (#1139).
