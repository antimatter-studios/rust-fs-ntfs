### Fixed

- **An in-place repair refuses a journal on a partition of the whole disk it repairs, or on the whole disk holding the partition it repairs (#948).** `repair_journal::check_journal_location` compared only the journal directory's `st_dev` with the target's `st_rdev`, so a journal on `/dev/disk2s1` was accepted while `/dev/disk2` was repaired. It now resolves both devices to their whole disk, through BSD names on macOS (a raw `rdisk` is the same device as its `disk`) and through `/sys/dev/block` on Linux, and refuses the same device, a partition of the target and the disk holding the target, each naming the relationship. The relationship is the pure `repair_journal::device_relation` over `DeviceRecord` values; another disk, or another partition of the target's disk, is still accepted.
